# Maryland early voting, 2026 vs. 2022 and 2024

Data and fact-check code behind Capital News Service graphics on mail-in and early in-person voting in Maryland's 2026 general election.

- `early_voting_factcheck_self.Rmd`: the fact check. It rebuilds every number in the graphics from the original state reports and writes the tables to `output/`.
- `Press Reports-selected/`: Maryland State Board of Elections daily reports for the 2022, 2024 and 2026 general elections, obtained through a public records request.
- `direct-download/`: 2026 reports downloaded from the board's website after the records request.
- `supplemental_reports/`: other files that fill gaps in the reports.
- `output/`: the fact-check tables and the CSVs uploaded to Flourish.
- `table_1_stacked_bars_d3.html`, `section_5_districts_stacked_bars_d3.html`: D3 versions of two graphics, served with GitHub Pages.

Graphics by David Landerman, Capital News Service.
