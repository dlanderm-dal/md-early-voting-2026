Congressional districts all see spike in early in-person voting

Early in-person voting is up 66% statewide as of a week before election day. District 4 had the largest spike with a 95% increase. While District 2, had a spike of 44%.

Figures refer to votes collected seven days before Election Day in each cycle, Nov. 1, 2022 and Oct. 27, 2026
Chart: David Landerman · Source: Maryland State Board of Elections mail-in and early-voting reports (2022 figures obtained through a public records request)
